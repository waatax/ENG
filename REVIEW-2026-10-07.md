# Mobile and English academy iteration

## Plan
The bilingual application remains the full school and exam library. Its listening flow needs direct exam choices, large targets, adjacent-set controls and a persistent player. Build an additional English-only academy at EN/ with the same learning cycle: instruction, worked examples, vocabulary and phrases, flashcards, listening, assessment, correction and saved progress. Use authored English explanations rather than unverified automatic translations. Start with two focused lessons per exam and expand only after content validation.

## Do
Implement independent English routes and storage; reuse the established visual language and link both editions. English speech reads the term, definition and example. Distinguish original practice from official examination content. Preserve the original bilingual corpus and tests.

## Check
Verify content integrity, English-only copy, route handling, speech cancellation, saved state, correction flow, touch target size and portrait overflow. Run existing regression tests and browser checks where available.

## Act
Record observed failures and fixes below before deployment. Future iterations should expand reviewed definitions and authentic worked examples, rather than imply that a small original practice bank predicts examination scores.

## Completed scope and evidence

All five courses provide explanations, worked examples, reflection notes, vocabulary, English speech, flashcards, recall marking, practice feedback, mistake review and local progress. This is an additional English teaching collection, not a translation of every original school lesson or all 3,500 exam cards. The bilingual library remains linked.
