## Findings and changes

1. Of 7,700 flashcards, 7,140 match four example templates from `scripts/flashcard_builders/common.py`. Examples such as “Professionals should punch all relevant data” are semantically invalid. That builder also synthesizes pronunciation by character substitution. The presentation adapter now withholds these cards' inferred IPA, chunks, memory tips, generic collocations and examples. Ten original replacement entries repair 13 cards across tiers. Original IDs, raw data and saved progress remain intact. The interface reports these counts. Unflagged cards are not automatically certified correct.
2. School-card scheduling previously advanced from 1 to 3 to 7 days on consecutive immediate answers. Early correct practice now preserves the current step and due date. Due reviews can advance; misses return after ten minutes. Existing stored schedules remain intact and the progress label no longer describes them as demonstrated mastery.
3. The flashcard front had no working flip button because the application delegates clicks to buttons. Added a real keyboard-operable button; the hidden face is inert and excluded from the accessibility tree. Search restores focus/caret and returns to the front.
4. The school spelling hint exposed the complete answer via its syllable field. Removed that answer leak. Word counts now derive from the data. Removed the unsupported complete-official-vocabulary coverage claim.
